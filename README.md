# Mi Proyecto 🚀
This folder contains two computation files performed in GAP on the search for skeletal chiral polyhedra and 4-polytopes whose edge graphs are contained in the 1-skeleta of the six regular convex 4-polytopes.
A Wythoff-like construction was used for rotary skeletal polyhedra and 4-polytopes based on distinguished rotational generators. Applying this construction, we classify all geometrically chiral polyhedra and chiral 4-polytopes with chiral facets whose 1-skeleta are contained in that of a regular convex 4-polytope. We prove that, up to isomorphism, there are exactly four such polyhedra and exactly three such 4-polytopes.
